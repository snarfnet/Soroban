Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Introduction

Japanese Soroban respects your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. All app functionality runs entirely on your device.

Motion Data

This app uses device motion sensors (accelerometer/gyroscope) to simulate gravity effects on the soroban beads. Motion data is processed locally in real-time and is never stored or transmitted.

Advertising

This app may display ads provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may collect anonymous usage data according to their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, please contact us through the App Store listing page.
