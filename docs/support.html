Japanese Soroban - Support

How to Use

The Japanese Soroban is a traditional abacus with 13 rods. Each rod has 1 heaven bead (worth 5) above the beam and 4 earth beads (worth 1 each) below.

  • Tap or slide beads toward the beam to activate them
  • Tap or slide beads away from the beam to deactivate them
  • The digital display shows the current value
  • Tilt your device to see gravity affect the beads
  • Tap the reset button to clear all beads

FAQ

Q: How do I read the value?

A: Each rod represents a decimal place. The rightmost rod is the ones place. Heaven beads pushed down = 5, earth beads pushed up = 1 each. The digital display at the top shows the total.

Q: The beads move when I tilt my phone

A: This is a feature! The app uses your device's motion sensors to simulate real gravity, just like a real soroban would behave when tilted.

Q: What devices are supported?

A: iPhone with iOS 15 or later (including iPhone 8 Plus and newer).
